Find your South Sudanese people.

Near you, or in the city you care about. JuNubin is a free social app for the South Sudanese community and diaspora — find, follow and message each other. Not a dating app.

How it works

See who's nearby on a privacy-first map (visibility is off by default, and always coarse), search the diaspora directory by city, profession, background and languages, and chat by text once you're connected. Strangers can only reach you through a message request you accept or decline.
