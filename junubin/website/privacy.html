JUNUBIN

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

1. Who We Are

JuNubin is a free social networking app for the South Sudanese community and diaspora — it helps you find, follow and message other South Sudanese people near you or in a city you care about. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights. By using JuNubin you agree to this Policy.

2. Data You Give Us

When you sign up: phone number or email, name, date of birth, gender, photos, and profile fields you choose to fill (e.g. city, background, languages, profession, interests, what you are open to). When you use the app: text messages you send, who you follow, message requests, reports you submit, and — only if you turn location visibility on — a coarse location accurate to roughly a kilometre. We do not collect precise location, we do not collect location in the background, and we never sell it or use it for ad targeting. JuNubin is free, so we collect no billing information at all.

3. Data Collected Automatically

Device type, OS version, app version, language, push-notification token, anonymous usage analytics (which screens you visit, errors), and approximate IP-based location. With your explicit permission only: camera (only to take profile photos or verify your identity), photo library (only to upload profile photos), contacts (only when you choose Find Friends). JuNubin never asks for microphone access and never reads precise device location — coordinates are coarsened before they are stored or shown.

4. How We Use Your Data

To create and protect your account; to power people discovery (the map and the Directory); to deliver messages and notifications; to detect fake profiles, fraud, and abuse; to comply with legal obligations; to improve the app through aggregated analytics. We do NOT sell your data to advertisers and we do NOT show third-party ads.

5. Who We Share Data With

Service providers acting on our behalf: Supabase (database + storage + auth), Expo (push notifications and over-the-air updates), and Apple/Google (push notification delivery). JuNubin is free — no payment processor receives data from the app. (Legacy pre-free-era subscriptions were processed by RevenueCat and Stripe; those providers retain only the records needed for their own legal obligations.) We share only what each provider needs to perform its function. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect user safety, or in connection with a merger or acquisition (you would be notified).

6. International Transfers

Your data may be processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate. By using JuNubin you consent to these transfers. We use providers that maintain appropriate safeguards (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses) for transfers from the EU/UK.

7. Data Retention

We keep account data while your account is active. When you delete your account, profile data, photos, and conversations are deleted within 30 days; some records (e.g. fraud signals, abuse reports) may be retained for up to seven years to meet legal, accounting, and safety obligations.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, or similar), you have the right to: (a) access the data we hold about you; (b) correct inaccurate data; (c) delete your data; (d) export a copy of your data; (e) object to or restrict certain processing; (f) withdraw consent for optional permissions (camera, location, contacts) at any time in your device settings. Email support@junubin.app to exercise any of these rights — we will respond within 30 days.

9. How to Delete Your Account

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. We will confirm by email/SMS and complete deletion within 30 days.

10. Security

We use TLS for data in transit, encrypted storage at rest via our cloud providers, row-level security on our database, hashed passwords, and one-time codes for sensitive actions. The app blocks screenshots and screen recording everywhere — content shared inside JuNubin cannot be captured by other users. No system is perfect; if we ever experience a data breach affecting you, we will notify you as required by law.

11. Children

JuNubin is for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, please email support@junubin.app and we will remove the account immediately.

12. Cookies and Web

If you visit junubin.app on the web (e.g. for Stripe checkout or password reset), we use only essential cookies needed to operate the page — no advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be communicated through the app and/or email at least 14 days before they take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change.

14. Contact Us

Privacy questions or rights requests: privacy@junubin.app or support@junubin.app. For users in the EU/UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.
