# JuNubin Community Guidelines

JuNubin is a space for the South Sudanese diaspora to meet, date,
and build community. These rules keep it safe and welcoming.
Violating them gets your content removed, your account restricted,
or in serious cases, banned.

> ⚠️ **Operator note**: this is the policy users see. Keep it concise
> and plain-spoken. Internal moderation thresholds live in
> [admin-runbook.md](./admin-runbook.md).

---

## ✅ Be yourself

- Use your **real first name**.
- Upload **photos of you**. Verified profiles get a checkmark.
- Be **18 or older**. Under-18 accounts are removed immediately.

## ✅ Be respectful

- Greet people the way you'd want to be greeted.
- Take "no" gracefully. One unanswered message is not an invitation
  to follow up five more times.
- Use language that doesn't punch down — no slurs about ethnicity,
  tribe, sexuality, gender, disability, religion, or politics.

## ✅ Stay safe

- Meet in public places.
- Tell a friend where you're going.
- Never send money to someone you haven't met in person — romance
  scams are real and we will not refund losses.
- Trust your instincts. Block + report anyone who makes you
  uncomfortable.

---

## ❌ What's not allowed

You'll be removed for:

### Inauthenticity
- Catfishing (photos that aren't you, deceased people, celebrities).
- Multiple accounts.
- Pretending to be a different age, gender, or ethnicity than you are.

### Harm
- Nude, sexually explicit, or violent imagery.
- Hate speech — slurs, supremacist language, targeted harassment by
  identity.
- Threats of violence, doxxing, stalking.
- Glorifying suicide, self-harm, or eating disorders.

### Commerce
- Sex work, escort services, or solicitation.
- Sales of goods, services, drugs, or weapons.
- Crypto, MLM, "investment opportunities", or signups for other
  platforms.

### Minors
- ANY sexual content involving someone under 18. Removed and reported
  to law enforcement.
- Photos that prominently feature your own minor children — for their
  safety.

### Illegal
- Anything that violates the law in your country or the country of
  the person you're interacting with.

## What happens when we receive a report

1. Our moderation team reviews within **24 hours** (most reports in
   under 6).
2. If we agree with the report:
   - First offence: content removed, warning sent.
   - Repeat or severe: account restricted (you can read but not
     match, follow, or message).
   - Most severe: account banned. We don't restore banned accounts.
3. If we disagree: we close the report and the reporter sees no
   action taken on their behalf.

You can appeal a moderation decision by emailing
[INSERT SUPPORT EMAIL] with subject "Moderation appeal". Include the
date of the decision and what you'd like reviewed.

## Reporting someone

- **From a profile**: tap the ⋯ menu → Report.
- **From a chat**: tap the ⋯ menu → Report.
- **From a post or comment**: tap the ⋯ menu → Report.

Reports are **confidential**. The person you reported never sees who
filed it.

## Photo verification

The blue ✓ next to a profile means we've checked the person is who
they say they are — they submitted a selfie that matches their photos.
It does **not** mean they're vetted, safe, or background-checked.
Treat verified the same as unverified: meet in public, take your
time, trust your gut.
